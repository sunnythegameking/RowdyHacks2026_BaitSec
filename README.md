# 🛡️ BaitSec

**Project Name:** BaitSec  
**Hackathon:** RowdyHacks XII  
**Challenge:** The Social Engineering Shield  

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Elderly and vulnerable individuals are targeted daily by sophisticated social engineering scams—ranging from fake IRS demands and romance scams to urgent phishing schemes[cite: 1]. **BaitSec** was created to act as a real-time defense shield against these predatory financial tactics.

Our solution implements active protection mechanisms—including AI-driven intent detection, deliberate mid-transaction friction points, and secondary trusted-contact approval workflows—to intercept and educate users before money changes hands.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

- **🤖 AI-Powered Scam Detection:** Real-time monitoring and analysis of suspicious payment requests, flagging high-risk indicators (e.g., urgency, gift card demands, unverified recipient details).
- **🛑 Smart Friction Points:** Introducing intentional delays and mid-scam intervention prompts that force users to pause, reassess, and verify details before processing transactions.
- **👥 Trusted-Contact Verification:** Automated escalation flows that require secondary authorization or review from a trusted contact/family member for high-risk payouts.
- **🎓 Mid-Scam Education:** Interactive, easy-to-understand contextual explanations shown directly on screen during flagged requests to help users spot tactics.

---

## 🏗️ How It Works

1. **Detection:** Incoming payment requests or transaction details are evaluated in real time for common social engineering markers.
2. **Intervention:** If high-risk indicators are triggered, the transaction is paused and presented with contextual warning screens.
3. **Escalation & Approval:** A notification is routed to a designated trusted contact to approve or deny the transfer.
